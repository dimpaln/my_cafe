About Radhe Cafe

Radhe Cafe is a friendly neighborhood cafe serving delicious and freshly prepared food.

Our menu includes popular Chinese favorites such as noodles, fried rice, Manchurian, spring rolls and crispy starters.

If you have a sweet tooth, our dessert collection includes chocolate cake, ice cream, brownies, gulab jamun and more.

We believe that great food, good service and a welcoming environment make every meal special.
